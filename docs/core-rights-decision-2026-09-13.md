# Historical Core ownership decision — superseded proposal

The 13 September proposal selected assignment of accepted new Core to a future
independent foundation. That was an ownership objective, not a finding that copyright
assignment is legally necessary for dual licensing. No transfer or contributor
agreement was executed by that document.

**Current proposal:** [retained copyright and an identified company or optional
foundation as licensor](licensing-model-v2.md). This is a deliberate revision after
the owner requested a company-compatible model. It is not a reinterpretation of
what the earlier decision said, nor a retroactive change to an executed agreement.
The public AGPL-3.0-or-later licence remains unchanged.

The [complete earlier decision at its immutable commit](https://github.com/joindaia/DAIA/blob/840298b5d4e3d6ce0279e10328e7b06f00170057/docs/core-rights-decision-2026-09-13.md)
is retained in Git history. The two [licensing](research/licensing-structure-review-2026-09-13.md)
and [Core-rights](research/core-rights-review-2026-09-13.md) reports remain archived
research, not operative contracts. Their source-mapping limitations remain relevant.

Retain the legal correction: under the amendment effective 1 January 2026, an
agreement for assignment or exclusive licensing must be written, while delivery
of copyright assignment still requires a deed. The current model links the primary
publications and separately requires counsel to validate nonexclusive grants,
future contributions, electronic assent, mandatory remedies and succession.
