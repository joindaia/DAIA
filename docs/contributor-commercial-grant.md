# Supplemental commercial grant — execution draft

**Execution draft: not an effective grant. Only a future independent DAIA foundation
may receive the commercial grant contemplated here. It must be formed, identified
and represented by an authorized signer before execution. The founder does not
receive this supplemental commercial authority personally.** This document records the proposed bargain. A PR,
agent signature, silence or contribution under AGPL does not execute this grant.
Existing contributions require separate, explicit assent by a competent rightsholder.

## Required agreement schedule

Before execution, identify the contributor/rightsholder, their signing authority
(including employer authority where applicable), the independent foundation and authorized
signer, effective date, exact covered contributions (commit and file/blob hashes),
excluded third-party material, final agreement version, applicable law and signatures.
Retain that schedule privately; publish only a nonidentifying clearance reference.
The generic name “DAIA project steward” is not a substitute for identifying a party.

## Proposed grant

The contributor retains the rights they hold and continues to license the covered
contributions under AGPL-3.0-or-later. In addition, to the extent of their authority,
they grant the identified foundation a worldwide, nonexclusive, royalty-free license
to reproduce, modify, distribute and sublicense those contributions as part of DAIA
under alternative commercial terms. No copyright assignment is required.

The additional grant is conditioned on keeping the covered contributions and the
foundation's commercially licensed core modifications available in an AGPL edition.
Commercial licensing must not discontinue that edition or revoke prior AGPL rights.
Revenue is allocated to project purposes under the agreed funding policy. The grant
does not require customers to publish their private changes or independent software.

An agreement must specify duration, remedies and termination for breach, treatment
of already granted customer sublicenses, and whether/how the grant can pass to an
identified successor that accepts the same obligations. These terms must be resolved
before signature; “irrevocable” must not be used to erase the community condition.

No rights are granted in excluded material, trademarks or third-party patents.
Any contributor patent grant needed for commercial distribution must be expressly
settled in the final agreement. The grant does not warrant that AI-generated output
is exclusive, copyrightable, original or free of third-party rights. Disclose source
and license information for imported or generated material; do not transfer rights
that belong to an employer or another party without their authorization.

The foundation's authorized signer must accept the community and funding obligations.
An agent cannot execute this agreement merely because it has a GitHub key or a
high reputation score. Copyright status and representation remain legal questions.

## Until execution

Contributions continue under the existing AGPL terms. Maintainers label their
commercial clearance **unverified**, **AGPL-only**, **excluded** or **cleared** with
supporting evidence; they must not treat this draft as a completed CLA. Accepting
an AGPL-only contribution does not block community work, but may require excluding
or replacing it in an alternative-licensed release. Never make a blanket commercial
promise about a release with unresolved rights.
