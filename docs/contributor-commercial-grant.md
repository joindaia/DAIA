# DAIA Core contributor agreement: v2 architecture

**Non-operative drafting instructions for Dutch IP counsel. Not an offer or an
agreement ready to accept.** No signature, copyright, patent or commercial permission
is created by this document, a worker registration or merging this PR. The
[current model](licensing-model-v2.md) permits a company or optional foundation;
retained copyright is the default, not assignment.

## 1. Parties and informed assent

Use an individual and an organizational schedule for the same substantive grant.
Identify the real rights holder and existing DAIA Licensor, legal jurisdiction,
registration/address where applicable, authorized signers, effective date, exact
agreement hash/version and accepted Core-scope version. Disclose whether the licensor
is founder-controlled and for-profit. Provide the complete instrument to each signer
on a durable medium; pseudonymous public participation does not replace private
identification. No agent or provider account is the contracting party.

Proposed plain-language acknowledgement: you keep your copyright; accepted,
specifically scoped Core work may also be licensed on proprietary terms by this
named counterparty; it may be a profit-making company; the complete official Core
stays AGPL; unrelated jobs are excluded. This acknowledgement supplements, not
replaces, the precise grant and evidence of assent.

## 2. Core and external work

Freeze the scope at admission: repository identity `1367256015` (`joindaia/DAIA`),
version `daia-core-v2`, and maintained coordinator, protocol, worker runtime,
security, tests, build/deployment infrastructure, website and project documentation.
Typical paths are `src/daia/`, `tests/`, `scripts/`, `deploy/`, `website/`, `docs/`,
`licensing/`, `.github/` and identified root project files. Paths alone do not
establish scope or rights. A later repository, component or materially enlarged
scope requires new express assent, not a unilateral policy update.

External customer/project/research jobs, other repositories, private output,
credentials, data and rejected/unused work are excluded, even if a worker or
storage path is shared. Imported dependencies and snippets remain separately
identified third-party material. Moving an external output into a Core directory
never grants rights. A rights holder must explicitly authorize a new Core submission
before reuse; original project obligations still apply.

## 3. Future contributions and workers

A master agreement may govern future qualifying contributions, but must not demand
all future works or impose a duty to produce work. Use a separately recorded,
revocable Core-participation authorization with specified workers, scope and a
maximum proposed 12-month duration. Renewal may reference the unchanged master
agreement without renegotiating past grants; no per-task signature is required.
Counsel must validate duration and the future-work mechanism, not merely its label.

Classify the task as Core before dispatch/work begins. Bind job scope, agreement
version and the legal participant's worker authorization before work, with no legal
identity or contract data disclosed to the worker. Direct human PRs use the same
eligibility test without a fabricated worker record. Work predating enrollment
requires a separately identified legacy submission schedule, not backdated assent.

Participants may stop future Core work and revoke an unaccepted submission. Check
revocation and employer-authority changes before both dispatch and acceptance;
withdrawn or expired authorization cannot be revived by a late merge. Do not make
the durability of already accepted grants depend on continued account access,
annual renewal or the participant's current employer.

## 4. Submission versus acceptance

The contributor knowingly submits material for evaluation/inclusion under
AGPL-3.0-or-later to the extent they may do so. This is separate from the supplemental
grant and does not assign copyright. Preserve notices and third-party restrictions.

The supplemental grant attaches only when an authorized maintainer formally accepts
the exact identified contribution **and** that material is incorporated into the
official Core within the authorized scope and participation period. Record base,
patch and resulting content hashes, contribution ID, exclusions, all relevant rights
holders and the inclusion event. A PR opening, label, worker signature or accidental
merge cannot substitute for these conditions. An accepted contribution's grant is
not lost merely because a later release removes it. New substantive integrator
changes need their own rights basis. Rejected/withdrawn output receives no supplemental
grant; any independently granted AGPL permissions retain their own effect.

## 5. Proposed copyright grant

To the extent relevant rights exist and the participant owns or controls authority
to license them, the participant grants the identified licensor a worldwide,
nonexclusive licence for the duration of those rights in each accepted covered
contribution, to reproduce, use, modify, adapt, prepare derivative works, distribute,
communicate and make available to the public, and publicly perform/display where
relevant to the type of work, in source and object form as part of DAIA Core and
products/services incorporating or derived from it.

Explicitly permit alternative commercial/proprietary sublicences, including
customer reproduction, modification, incorporation into proprietary software,
distribution and further sublicensing within that defined scope. The licensor may
charge fees for these rights. This is not an independent permission to sell the
participant's unrelated work or a free-standing transferable IP portfolio.
Ordinary customer sublicensing and qualified succession are separate permissions.

The intended grant is without contractual royalties or an automatic share of licence
revenue. The licensor's enforceable open-Core undertakings are part of the bargain.
**Preserve mandatory statutory remuneration and remedies; do not purport to waive
them.** If the required legal analysis calls for additional compensation, settle it
before activation. Separately commissioned paid work uses its own payment schedule.
The grant is not terminable at will after acceptance, subject to mandatory law and
the express material-breach remedies below. 'Irrevocable' is not an absolute waiver.

No copyright assignment, trademark/passing-off permission, confidentiality waiver,
right to provider accounts or unrelated database/other IP right is implied. Specify
any additional necessary transferable right separately rather than using 'all IP'.
Contributors remain free to use and separately license their own rights; that is not
authority to relicense other contributors' work or imply official endorsement.

## 6. Licensor's community covenant

The licensor accepts the open-Core obligations in the current model in its own
signature. Publish the accepted contribution and every licensor-owned/controlled
Core improvement used in official releases, official hosted Core or commercial
Core deliveries, with complete corresponding source under AGPL-3.0-or-later no
later than that use/release. No permanent closed official Core, affiliate diversion,
'enterprise' relabeling or scope-list deletion may evade this obligation. Preserve
previous AGPL permissions. Do not promise exclusive rights in otherwise open code.

Customers may keep their own permitted private modifications and independent
software closed. The agreement does not capture their work or require upstream
submission. Contract ahead of commissioned work about who owns it and whether it
will enter official Core; customer secrecy cannot excuse withholding Core which
the licensor already controls and is obliged to publish.

## 7. Breach, cure and customer certainty

Proposed remedy: a contributor may notify a material breach with enough particulars
to investigate. Allow 60 days to cure unless urgent injunctive relief is justified;
this is a proposed contractual period, not a statutory safe harbour. During a
substantiated open-Core breach, suspend new licences/expansions of the affected
rights. If uncured, permit termination of the affected supplemental authority and
performance/damages remedies as allowed by law. No unilateral termination simply
because ownership of shares changes or the project becomes profitable.

Expressly preauthorize survival of bona-fide customer sublicences validly granted
before suspension/termination, within their original paid-for/permitted scope,
including agreed private modifications and previously authorized downstream grants.
Do not expand surviving rights through a sham bulk licence or post-termination
renewal that actually adds new scope. Customer nonpayment, customer breach, patent
retaliation and claims from an actual third-party owner remain separate matters.

Counsel must implement the enforceable downstream-survival/direct-fallback mechanism
in both the contributor and customer instruments, including grantor incapacity,
insolvency and succession. An intention that licences survive is insufficient.
Survival never cures a grant the participant had no authority to make. Public AGPL
permissions are governed separately and are not conditional on this commercial dispute.

## 8. Patents

Include a nonexclusive, worldwide, royalty-free patent licence for claims the
participant can license, necessarily infringed by the contribution alone or by
its combination with the identified Core to which it was submitted. Permit make,
have made, use, offer for sale, sell, import and otherwise transfer, and the
necessary customer/downstream sublicensing. Limit coverage to those necessary
claims; do not demand the employer's entire patent portfolio or unknown third-party
rights. Record whether the organizational signer controls the relevant authority.

Use narrowly drafted defensive termination for a recipient bringing an offensive
patent claim against the covered contribution/Core. Have counsel delimit necessary
defensive counterclaims and isolate consequences to that recipient, not innocent
customers. Preserve any independently applicable AGPL patent permissions. No
participant warrants freedom from all third-party patent claims or must fund an
unlimited patent search. This is a design informed by Apache/Qt, not their verbatim
agreement or a promise of patent clearance.

## 9. Authority, AI and proportionate assurances

The signer confirms their capacity, authority to grant the identified rights,
authorization of the listed workers, and accuracy of disclosures to their knowledge
after reasonable enquiry. Employer-funded accounts or hardware alone establish
neither individual ownership nor organizational authority. Use an organizational
signer/authorization where needed; contractors and joint authors require their
actual chain of authority. Freeze future clearance when ownership/employment changes.

Disclose known copied material, licence restrictions and meaningful AI-generation
provenance. Purely generated output may lack copyright; provider contractual output
allocation does not prove copyrightability, originality or exclusivity. The grant
operates only on rights actually held/controlled. No fabricated human authorship,
blanket noninfringement warranty, duty to search all patents, or unlimited contributor
indemnity. Agree liability limits subject to mandatory law and deliberate misconduct.
Unresolved provenance prevents commercial clearance, even if the work may be used
under an adequate public licence. Minors require a separately reviewed lawful route;
otherwise do not admit them to this grant. Address moral rights through limited
lawful consents, not a fictitious worldwide assignment of non-transferable rights.

## 10. Enforcement and missing participants

Expressly authorize proportionate enforcement of covered rights where legally
permitted, including notices and settlements which do not reduce retained contributor
rights or waive mandatory remedies. Allocate proceeds, costs, conflict handling and
control of proceedings. Do not create an unlimited duty to participate at personal
expense. A specific litigation mandate or assignment of an accrued claim may be
needed under the law/forum concerned; a nonexclusive licence does not automatically
create universal standing. Validate survival of authorizations separately from the
copyright licence. The design removes routine post-hoc relicensing consent, not
every possible need for evidence or participation in litigation.

## 11. Qualified successors and other terms

Apply the whole-position successor requirements in the current model, including
actual signed accession, advance cooperation where valid, contributor enforceability,
notice, privacy-compliant evidence transfer and no retained parallel official
licensing power. The company route must be explicit in the original agreement;
old foundation-only grants stay outside unless validly changed. Do not promise
creditor-proof rights using a contractual no-pledge clause.

Specify Dutch governing law and a suitable forum without displacing mandatory
international protections. Define notices, privacy purposes/retention, severability
without broadening grants, and precedence: executed schedule and accepted immutable
version govern; website edits and automatic agent agreement do not amend them.
Material enlargement requires new express assent. There is no employment, partnership,
provider authorization, shareholding or guaranteed acceptance/payment by signing.
