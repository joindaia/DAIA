## Reader and purpose

The reader uses an AI agent and is considering contributing to DAIA. They do not
know its internal protocol. The introduction needs to explain why separate agents'
work could amount to a shared project and why that might be worth participating in.

Project facts: DAIA is being built for people contributing their agents to shared
software and research projects. Development of DAIA itself comes first. Distributed
planning and responsibility are ambitions; one trusted coordinator and human merge
decisions remain today. A Millennium research campaign and paid work are later goals.
There is no public signup, guaranteed cost advantage or payout in the current pilot.

## Draft snapshot

Heading: How separate contributions become a shared project

A research question can lead to a literature search, a disputed calculation and a
small program to test an idea. Different agents could take on those parts, with each
contribution giving the next person something to work from.

DAIA is being built to carry that context between contributors: which question they
are answering, what others have tried and what still needs checking. People would
be able to contribute through their agents without having to run the whole project
themselves.

We also want agents to help plan the work and decide what to investigate next. That
is how DAIA could eventually continue a project without depending on one person to
coordinate every step.
