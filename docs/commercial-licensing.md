# Commercial licensing and project funding

**Design selected by the owner on 14 September 2026: retained contributor copyright,
with an identified company or
optional foundation as official licensor. Commercial licences are not offered and
no contributor agreement is activated by these documents.** The
[current assessment and model](licensing-model-v2.md) supersedes the mandatory
foundation/assignment proposal. The AGPL licence itself is unchanged.

## What is, and is not, being sold

DAIA's complete official Core remains AGPL-3.0-or-later. Commercial use, paid hosting
and forks are permitted under its terms. Use at work or within one legal person does
not itself require payment. Modified network interaction must be assessed under
section 13, including relevant internal network users; distribution/conveying has
other obligations. Separate subsidiaries are not automatically the same legal person.
Contractor arrangements require their own assessment, including section 2 where
applicable. Do not market an alternative licence as mandatory for ordinary business
use. Read [the actual licence](../LICENSE), not just this summary.

An alternative licence may permit defined proprietary incorporation, distribution
or closed modifications of specifically cleared DAIA code. Specify versions,
artifacts, affiliates, contractors, OEM/distribution rights and downstream rights
in the customer agreement. It cannot waive third-party obligations or grant rights
DAIA does not have. Services, support and hosted infrastructure may be sold separately
without changing the software licence. External job outputs follow their own terms.

## Licensor and complete open Core

A legally identified, existing DAIA Licensor may be a founder-owned company; an
independent foundation is an option, not a requirement. A project name, account,
trade name or prospective company is not an incorporated counterparty. The founder
retains rights actually held pending a separate [legacy instrument](founder-and-successor-grants.md).
Share ownership alone grants no personal authority over contributor code.

The licensor must accept enforceable obligations to publish the same accepted Core
and its own controlled Core improvements used in official releases, hosted Core or
commercial deliverables under AGPL, with complete corresponding source, no later
than that use/release. No permanent superior closed official Core branch or affiliate
bypass. Customers may keep their own licensed private modifications closed; unrelated
customer code, external jobs, secrets and data are not captured. The
[supplemental grant architecture](contributor-commercial-grant.md) describes the
scope, remedies and protection of already valid customer sublicences.

## Funding without a misleading nonprofit promise

Under the proposed **company route**, receipts may fund development, infrastructure,
audits, legal work, bounties, staff and contractors. The company may earn profit and
make lawful shareholder distributions. Contributors must explicitly receive this
information before granting commercial rights. No fixed founder share, salary,
dividend or payment is authorized by this proposal. Work compensation and distributions
need their own lawful decisions, accounting, conflict handling and tax review.

Under an optional **foundation route**, project-purpose use, independent governance,
no self-approved compensation and nonprofit succession must be embodied in that
entity's instruments. Do not advertise those guarantees for a founder-controlled
company. Existing donor restrictions, host terms and signed foundation-only instruments
remain binding according to their terms; this proposal does not rewrite them.

A fiscal host may receive/administer funds under an explicit agreement. That does
not make it the IP owner, commercial licensor, employer or customer-contracting party.
Appoint each role separately if needed; no host is appointed here.

## Preconditions for any alternative licence

Require an existing identified licensor and authorized signatory; executed applicable
individual/organizational and founder grants; a rights review of the **whole exact
release** including dependencies and inherited material; publication of the matching
AGPL Core; reviewed customer terms; and lawful contracting/accounting arrangements.
Unknown or AGPL-only included material blocks a blanket proprietary exception.
Compatible third-party material retains its own licences and notices.

The [rights-register design](licensing-rights-register.md) separates private evidence
from public references. Its offline preview is not legal clearance or a deployed
release gate. `licensing/program.json` records an inactive design state; changing a
JSON flag cannot sign a contract or authorize commercial licensing.
