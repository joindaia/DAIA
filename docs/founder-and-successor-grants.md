# Founder, staff and successor instruments

**Counsel drafting schedules, not executed grants.** Read with the
[current model](licensing-model-v2.md) and [contributor grant](contributor-commercial-grant.md).

## Founder to identified licensor

Use a separate schedule for existing material. Identify the legal founder/rightsholder,
existing legal recipient and authorized signatories privately. Inventory exact historical
commits, files, inherited material and exclusions. Document employer, contractor,
joint-author, copied and generated provenance. A repository name, sole Git author
or AI provider's output allocation does not prove title to every byte.

Prefer the same nonexclusive, worldwide commercial copyright/patent grant, only to
the extent of rights actually held/controlled. It must explicitly permit the planned
customer sublicences and bind the recipient to the complete AGPL Core covenant,
remedies, customer survival and qualified succession. Record duration, consideration
and mandatory author protections. No retroactive signature or claim that a future
contribution agreement already covered historical work. A separately chosen assignment
requires its own proper instrument/deed and a considered licence-back; it is not the
default merely because the contributor is the founder.

Founder's own retained rights do not create a parallel official licence programme
for other contributors' code. For founder-controlled official Core derivatives, apply
the same open-Core covenant without a personal/affiliate escape. Future founder,
employee and contractor contributions also need an adequate rights basis. Share
ownership, payment and job title are not substitutes for that chain. Company-controlled
Core improvements must not be diverted into a closed branch through another entity.

## Whole-position succession schedule

Before an asset/contract transfer, record and execute all required acts for:

1. An existing, identified company or foundation with authorized signatory, operational
   capacity and responsibility for the DAIA programme.
2. Written accession to every applicable contributor obligation, immutable agreement
   versions, complete AGPL availability and contributor remedies. No unilateral weakening.
3. The actual transferred rights, contractual positions and associated claims, with
   advance cooperation/consent only to the extent effective under applicable law.
4. Notice to contributors, preservation of their enforceable rights and privacy-compliant
   transfer of accessible evidence; independent copies of the source and records.
5. Continuing valid customer sublicences, support obligations where assumed and clear
   allocation of pre-transfer liabilities without depriving third parties of claims.
6. No detached sale of unrestricted commercial rights, no broader creditor permission,
   and no retained parallel official sublicensing authority for the former licensor.
7. Binding the successor to equivalent onward-transfer requirements. A company may
   qualify only where the original instruments allow that destination.
8. A dissolution/failure plan: find a qualified successor, preserve public source and
   evidence, otherwise stop new alternative grants without inventing a founder fallback.

A share sale ordinarily leaves the contracting company unchanged; preserve its
obligations and notify a control change. It does not retrospectively expand a grant.
An asset sale/contract transfer has different legal requirements. Insolvency, statutory
succession and enforcement of no-transfer/no-pledge provisions need specific advice.
No bankruptcy-proof estate, automatic novation or irrevocable power is asserted here.

The rights registry should retain executed instruments and dated qualification
review. Updating a public role or repository owner is not execution of this schedule.
